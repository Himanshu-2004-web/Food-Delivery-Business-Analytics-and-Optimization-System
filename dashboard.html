Prescriptive Analytics: Evidence-Based Business Actions

Data-driven operational interventions to eliminate delivery latency, protect platform commission margins, and reduce customer churn.

⚡ 1. Dynamic Fleet Surge
Trigger: Dinner peak (19:00 - 21:30) experiences a 16.1% late delivery rate in Bangalore & Mumbai.
Action: Deploy a 20%-25% delivery rider surge bonus to increase active driver density during evening peak hours.
📍 2. Distance Radius Throttling
Trigger: Deliveries > 10 km take 60.6 mins on average and crash customer ratings to 2.08 stars.
Action: Throttle standard delivery radius to 8 km during monsoons and peak traffic; charge ₹15/km surcharge for long routes.
🍳 3. Kitchen Prep SLA
Trigger: Over 50% of delay time at high-volume Biryani & Italian partners originates from kitchen prep delays.
Action: Enforce an 18-minute kitchen preparation SLA; mandate 14-day quality reviews for kitchens with < 3.8 stars.
💰 4. Subsidy Margin Protection
Trigger: Free delivery subsidies on low-ticket orders (< ₹300) wipe out over 40% of platform commission.
Action: Restrict free delivery subsidies to basket sizes exceeding a minimum order value of ₹550.
🎁 5. Automated Compensation
Trigger: Exactly 55 orders breached the statistical IQR Upper Bound (55.9 mins), triggering severe churn.
Action: Program an automated ₹75 platform wallet credit trigger whenever an order exceeds 56 mins.
